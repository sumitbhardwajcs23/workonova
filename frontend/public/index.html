FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers, without the fine print.

How many revisions are included?+

Every service package includes the stated revision rounds—typically two to three standard rounds, or unlimited revisions where explicitly specified. Revisions cover changes within the original approved brief; new features or major brief changes are quoted separately.

When are final files and source code delivered?+

Final high-resolution assets, editable source files, and software source code are released after full payment is complete. On settlement, you receive commercial ownership and usage rights for the final approved deliverables.

Can I cancel a project or request a refund?+

A written cancellation submitted within 12 hours of booking—before work begins or a specialist is assigned—qualifies for a full refund less payment-gateway fees. Once work has started, any eligible refund is calculated according to completed milestones. Final files, approved milestones, and delays caused by missing client assets are non-refundable.

How is my project information protected?+

Client briefs, brand assets, proprietary code, and unreleased marketing materials are handled under strict non-disclosure obligations. We use secure storage, encryption, and restricted access protocols, and we never sell or trade your personal data.

How can I request data deletion or a refund?+

Email contact@workonova.com or WhatsApp +91 7983264117 with your Order ID, client name, and request. Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5–7 business days; you may also request access, correction, or deletion of your stored personal data.
